Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Rippl is a tool that publishes one video to every social platform you connect, and turns long livestreams into short clips. This policy describes exactly what data that involves, where it goes, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Rippl is an early-stage service run directly by its founders. The practices below describe the service as it actually works today, not as we intend it to work at scale.

Who we are

Rippl is operated by its founders. For any privacy question, correction or deletion request, contact umutboi43@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 days.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies other than the session cookie above.
  • No contact lists, follower data, direct messages, or the content of your social accounts beyond the posts Rippl itself creates.
  • No card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; Rippl never sees or stores your card number.

How your video is processed

Audio separation and analysis for uploads run on our own servers using open-source models (Demucs for source separation, DeepFilterNet for noise reduction).

When you clip a stream, its speech is transcribed on our own servers (Whisper). To choose the clips and write their titles, captions and on-screen text, we send the transcript text, short excerpts of the stream's public chat, and the stream's title to Anthropic's Claude API. No video or audio is sent. Anthropic processes this under its commercial API terms and does not use it to train its models. Neither do we: your videos are not used to train any model.

Where it is stored, and how it is protected

Your data is held in a database and on disk on servers we control. Access credentials are stored so that scheduled publishing can run without you present. We protect it as follows:

  • Encryption in transit. Every connection between your browser and Rippl, and between Rippl and YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Stripe and Anthropic, uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Platform credentials are encrypted at rest. The OAuth refresh tokens, access tokens and client secrets we store for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram are encrypted in our database with authenticated encryption (Fernet: AES-128 with an HMAC-SHA256 integrity check). The encryption key is kept separately from the database, so a copy of the database or of a backup alone does not reveal any credential. Database backups contain the same encrypted values.
  • Encrypted storage. The server's disk, where your videos, clips and database are kept, is protected with full-disk encryption (FileVault, AES-XTS).
  • Credentials stay out of logs. Tokens are removed from our server logs before they are written.
  • Account isolation. Every request is checked against your session, and Rippl refuses access to any video, clip, post or credential that belongs to another account. Your session cookie is random, sent only over HTTPS (Secure), unreadable to scripts (HttpOnly), and we store only a SHA-256 hash of it, never the cookie itself.
  • Signed, expiring media links. Links to your rendered files carry a cryptographic signature, and the links handed to Instagram expire after 6 hours.
  • Least privilege. For YouTube, Rippl asks only for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It cannot read, change or delete your existing videos, comments or other channel data. View counts for clips you post are read from YouTube's public data, not with your credentials.
  • Limited access. Only Rippl's founders can access the servers, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google user data

Rippl's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube access only to upload the videos you ask Rippl to publish. We do not transfer Google user data to anyone except YouTube itself, do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not let any person read it except with your permission, for security purposes, or where the law requires.

Being straight with you about early-stage security. Each browser has its own Rippl account. There are no passwords yet, so clearing your cookies or switching browsers starts a new, empty account. Please revoke access (see below) if you stop using Rippl.

Your choices

  • Disconnect a platform. Revoking Rippl's access stops us publishing there. You can also revoke directly in your Google account, TikTok settings, or Meta Business settings — that works even if Rippl is unreachable.
  • Delete your data. Use Delete my data at the bottom of the Rippl app. It immediately removes your clips, uploads, renders, stream history, scheduled posts, stored platform credentials and account, except the payment records described above. Any unused credit balance is lost. You can also email us and we will do it for you within 30 days.
  • Get a copy. Ask and we will send you what we hold.
  • Correct it. Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it.

If you are in the UK, EU or another region with statutory data rights, these apply to you regardless of this policy, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rippl is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used Rippl, contact us and we will delete the account.
